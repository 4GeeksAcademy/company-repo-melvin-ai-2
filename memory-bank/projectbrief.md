# Project Brief

## Business
Brasaland is a 14-location grilled food restaurant chain serving Colombia and Florida. Its brand depends on consistent food quality, warm service, and fast preparation across two countries.

## Problem
Customer and operational information is fragmented. The public website needs to represent the complete brand and capture Brasa Points registrations, while internal teams need visible, reliable metrics from reusable business logic.

## Objectives
- Deliver a responsive public website with the complete Milestone 1 content and loyalty flow.
- Deliver a separate backoffice that visibly demonstrates Milestone 2 operations calculations.
- Keep shared domain logic in one canonical monorepo location.
- Give coding agents durable context, rules, and a repeatable delivery process.

## Users
- Customers discovering Brasaland locations and joining Brasa Points.
- Restaurant and corporate operators reviewing sales, waste, and performance.
- Developers and agents extending the monorepo in later milestones.

## Success
Both Next.js applications run independently, the website is complete and reusable, the backoffice imports rather than duplicates business logic, and every change can be verified through the repository delivery workflow.
